# Graphics and render options

Five profiles change runtime render pacing and physics cost: Ultra, Premium, Balanced, Gaming and Eco. Ultra targets 120 FPS and defaults to 60 Hz physics. Solver frequency is independently selectable. Supersampling at 1.5× or 2× is constrained by a six-million-pixel budget and device limits.

The permission-gated material layer offers reversible toon shift/toony/GI/outline adjustments and limited anisotropy. It preserves original values and texture filtering on rollback. Source, Soft Anime and Premium R2 are options on editable fixtures. The restricted Leva source retains its materials. Scene lighting is independent of asset editing.

No new high-resolution texture maps, normal/roughness data, mesh detail or corrective facial geometry were generated for the source VRM. Higher render resolution improves sampled edges, not source texture information. Ray tracing remains false in renderer telemetry; there is no drop-in ray-traced MToon/VRM implementation in this candidate.

The observed GPU is displayed separately from the requested preference. The current browser used RTX 5070 even when RTX 4050 was requested. 120 FPS is a target, not a guarantee on every device or refresh rate.
